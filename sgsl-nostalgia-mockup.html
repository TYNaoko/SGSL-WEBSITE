Singapore Sign Language (SgSL) developed within Singapore’s Deaf community and has been shaped by several influences, including Shanghainese Sign Language and American Sign Language.

"Similar to spoken languages, signed languages develop independently based on geographical location and events occurring to the community" -SGSL SignBank

Today it has its own signs, including ones for Singlish words. In 2018 SGSL was officially adopted in signing-desiginated schools!
